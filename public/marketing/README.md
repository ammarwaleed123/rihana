# Marketing photos

These are **free-license placeholder photos** (from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com),
which requires no attribution and permits commercial use) so the public site
looks and feels like a real nursery during development.

**Replace them with the nursery's own photos** before launch — keep the same
filenames and the site picks them up with no code change:

| File | Used for | Suggested replacement |
|---|---|---|
| `hero.jpg` | Home hero | A bright, happy moment — children and an educator |
| `care.jpg` | "A gentle place" | An educator giving warm one-to-one attention |
| `reading.jpg` | Programs / story time | Story time or a reading corner |
| `art.jpg` | A day at Rihana | Painting / craft table |
| `music.jpg` | A day at Rihana | Music or circle time |
| `movement.jpg` | A day at Rihana | Active / movement play |
| `calm.jpg` | A day at Rihana | Quiet / rest time |
| `play.jpg` | A day at Rihana | Imaginative / role play |

Use photos you have **consent** to publish. Enrolled children's day-to-day
photos belong in the private family portal, never here on the public site.
Landscape shots around 1200–1600px wide work best; keep files reasonably small.
